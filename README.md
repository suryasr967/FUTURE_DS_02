## 📌 Project Overview

Customer retention is an important factor for the growth and profitability of subscription-based businesses. This project analyzes customer and subscription data to understand churn patterns, customer retention, and factors associated with customers leaving a service.

The analysis focuses on identifying customer segments with higher churn, understanding customer lifetime patterns, and deriving actionable insights that can help businesses improve customer retention.

---

## 🎯 Business Questions

The analysis aims to answer the following questions:

- Why are customers leaving the platform?
- Which customer segments are most likely to churn?
- How long do customers typically stay active?
- Which customer characteristics and subscription factors are associated with churn?
- What actions can improve customer retention?

## 🔍 Analysis Performed

The project includes analysis of:

### 1. Customer Churn

Analyzed the overall distribution of customers who churned and those who remained active.

### 2. Churn by Customer Segment

Compared churn patterns across different customer characteristics and service categories.

### 3. Contract & Subscription Analysis

Analyzed how different subscription and contract types are associated with customer churn.

### 4. Customer Tenure

Studied customer tenure to understand how long customers typically remain with the service and how churn varies with tenure.

### 5. Payment & Billing Analysis

Examined payment methods and billing-related factors to identify patterns associated with customer churn.

### 6. Customer Lifetime Patterns

Analyzed customer tenure and subscription characteristics to understand customer lifetime behavior.

## 💡 Key Business Insights

- Customer churn varies across different customer and subscription segments.
- Customers with shorter service tenure show different churn behavior compared with long-term customers.
- Contract and subscription characteristics have an important relationship with customer retention.
- Certain payment and billing patterns are associated with higher churn.
- Understanding early-stage customer behavior can help businesses identify customers who may be at risk of leaving.

---

## 💼 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following actions can help improve customer retention:

- Identify and engage customers who show early signs of churn.
- Improve onboarding and customer support during the initial stages of the customer lifecycle.
- Encourage customers to move toward longer-term subscription plans.
- Provide targeted retention offers to high-risk customer segments.
- Monitor customer satisfaction and service usage regularly.
- Use customer data to develop personalized retention strategies.
